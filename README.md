# 13 November 2026 — The World in One Page

A make.fun news-image-01 entry: one image that sums up Friday 13 November
2026's world news, drawn as a newspaper front page. The masthead reads
"13 NOVEMBER 2026"; below it, three panels carry the day's most
consequential stories, each with a short caption. The program (`main.ts`)
copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`.

The figures in the peace-talks panel are generic officials, not portraits
of any real person; the flags (Ukraine, United States, European Union) are
the story's own.

## The three stories on the page

1. **Tankers hit in Hormuz** — Iran's Revolutionary Guards said on 13
   November that they destroyed a U.S. early-warning radar in Bahrain, and
   that four oil tankers were hit by explosions in the Strait of Hormuz.
   (Just Security Early Edition, 13 Nov 2026; Zark News Daily Briefing.)
2. **China drills off Taiwan** — Taiwanese security officials told Reuters
   on 13 November that China had conducted an extensive, unannounced
   military exercise across the western Pacific and around Taiwan earlier
   in November. (The Raisina Post, 13 Nov 2026.)
3. **Europe joins Ukraine talks** — U.S. envoys Jared Kushner and Steve
   Witkoff met President Vladimir Putin in Moscow, then briefed President
   Volodymyr Zelensky in Ireland; Russia and Ukraine agreed to expand the
   next round of peace talks to include European nations. (Zark News Daily
   Briefing; BBC via SatRecon World Brief, 13 Nov 2026.)

## How to run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
